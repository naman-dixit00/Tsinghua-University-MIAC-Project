## 📌 Overview

This repository documents participation and insights associated with the **Future Learning with AI: Higher Education Learning Innovation** program, hosted by **Tsinghua University**. 

The initiative aims to bring together an international cohort of innovators to shape the paradigm of the **"Future University"** through cutting-edge learning technologies, artificial intelligence integration, and novel pedagogical frameworks.

## 🏛️ Organizing Body

* **Institution:** Tsinghua University (清华大学)
* **Academic Host:** School of Education, Tsinghua University
* **Initiative:** Global Recruitment — First Cohort of *"Learning Experience Ambassadors"*
* **Core Vision:** *Shape the "Future University" with Us*

## 🎯 Program Focus Areas

* **AI-Driven Pedagogy:** Integration of artificial intelligence to personalize and optimize higher education environments.
* **Learning Experience Innovation:** Redefining student-centric interactions, cognitive engagement, and modern educational workflows.
* **Global Collaboration:** Building scalable solutions for global academic institutions and future-oriented learning environments.
